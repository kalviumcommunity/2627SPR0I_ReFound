# Campus Lost and Found

### Lost something on campus? Find it here.

## Problem Statement

Students cannot tell whether an item lost on campus has already been found. Campus Lost and Found solves this problem by providing a digital lost-and-found register where desk volunteers can record found items and mark completed handovers.

Students can browse non-sensitive descriptions of found items to identify their belongings. Private owner-verification details are kept confidential and are never displayed publicly. Unclaimed item records are stored persistently so they remain available even after service updates.
